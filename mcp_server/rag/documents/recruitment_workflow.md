# Introduction

This document defines Talenta's official recruitment workflow. Every recruitment request follows these standardized steps to ensure consistency, transparency, and compliance with company hiring policies.

---

# WF-101 Job Request

The recruitment process begins when a department submits a hiring request.

The request must include:

- Job title
- Department
- Required degree
- Minimum experience
- Required skills
- Hiring priority

Only approved job requests may proceed to recruitment.

---

# WF-102 Candidate Screening

Recruiters review incoming applications.

The screening process verifies:

- Education
- Experience
- Required skills
- Resume quality

Candidates that clearly fail mandatory requirements may be rejected immediately.

Qualified candidates proceed to skill matching.

---

# WF-103 Skill Matching

The AI Recruitment Assistant compares:

- Candidate skills
- Job skills
- Experience
- Education

A match score is generated.

Score Interpretation

90–100 → Excellent Match

75–89 → Qualified

60–74 → Manual Review

Below 60 → Reject

Candidates with acceptable scores move to interviews.

---

# WF-104 Interview Scheduling

Qualified candidates are invited for interviews.

Recruiters must:

- Contact the candidate
- Schedule interview dates
- Assign interviewers
- Confirm attendance

Interview invitations should be sent within three business days.

---

# WF-105 Interview Evaluation

Interviewers submit structured feedback after every interview.

Required information:

- Technical score
- Communication score
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Recommendation

Incomplete evaluations prevent hiring decisions.

---

# WF-106 HR Review

After interviews are completed, HR reviews:

- Interview results
- Match score
- Recruiter notes
- Background verification

HR may:

- Approve
- Reject
- Request another interview
- Request manual review

---

# WF-107 Offer Letter

Offer letters are generated only if:

- Candidate passed all interview stages.
- Background verification is successful.
- HR Manager approved the application.

Recruiters cannot generate offer letters independently.

---

# WF-108 Candidate Status

Every application must be assigned one of the following statuses:

PENDING

SHORTLISTED

INTERVIEW

HR REVIEW

APPROVED

REJECTED

HIRED

Status updates must accurately reflect the candidate's progress.

---

# WF-109 Onboarding

After the candidate accepts the offer:

HR prepares:

- Employment contract
- Employee account
- Orientation schedule
- Equipment request

The recruitment workflow officially ends after onboarding preparation.
