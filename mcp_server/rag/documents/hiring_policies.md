# Introduction

This document contains Talenta Partners Group's official hiring policies. All recruiters, hiring managers, and HR specialists must follow these policies during the recruitment process.

---

# HR-101 Experience Requirement

Every candidate must satisfy the minimum experience requirement defined in the job description.

Rules:

- Candidates with experience below the required minimum cannot receive automatic approval.
- Applications failing this requirement should either be rejected or escalated for manual HR review.
- Experience is measured in completed professional years.

---

# HR-102 Education Requirement

Candidates should possess an educational background related to the position.

Examples of acceptable matches:

- Computer Science → Software Engineer
- Information Systems → Data Analyst
- Information Security → Cybersecurity Engineer

Examples requiring manual review:

- Business Administration → Software Engineer
- Marketing → Cybersecurity Engineer

---

# HR-103 Match Score Policy

Candidate suitability is measured using a match score.

Score Categories:

90–100 : Excellent Match

75–89 : Qualified Candidate

60–74 : Manual HR Review Required

Below 60 : Reject

The match score is calculated using:

- Technical skills
- Years of experience
- Education
- Job requirements

---

# HR-104 Background Verification

Before sending an official offer, the following checks are mandatory:

- Employment history verification
- Education verification
- Identity verification
- Reference checks

Failure in any mandatory verification pauses the hiring process until HR completes an investigation.

---

# HR-105 Final Hiring Approval

Only HR Managers have authority to approve final hiring decisions.

Recruiters may:

- Review applications
- Schedule interviews
- Recommend candidates

Recruiters may NOT:

- Send final offers
- Approve hiring independently

---

# HR-106 Candidate Confidentiality

Candidate information is confidential.

The following information must never be shared outside the recruitment team:

- Personal phone numbers
- Email addresses
- Salary expectations
- Recruiter notes
- Interview evaluations

---

# HR-107 Equal Opportunity Policy

Talenta evaluates every candidate fairly.

Employment decisions must never consider:

- Gender
- Nationality
- Religion
- Age
- Disability

Selection is based only on qualifications and job requirements.

---

# HR-108 Offer Letter Policy

Official offer letters are generated only after:

- All interviews are completed.
- Background verification succeeds.
- HR Manager approval is recorded.

Any missing requirement prevents offer generation.

---

# HR-109 Application Status Definitions

PENDING

Application is under review.

SHORTLISTED

Candidate passed initial screening.

INTERVIEW

Candidate is currently in interview stages.

APPROVED

Candidate received final HR approval.

REJECTED

Candidate failed one or more hiring requirements.

HIRED

Candidate accepted the offer and joined the company.
