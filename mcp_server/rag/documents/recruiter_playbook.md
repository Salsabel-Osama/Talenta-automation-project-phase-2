# Introduction

This playbook provides practical guidance for recruiters during the recruitment process. It complements Talenta's official hiring policies by explaining best practices, communication standards, and candidate evaluation techniques.

Recruiters should use this guide during resume screening, interviews, and candidate communication.

---

# RP-101 Resume Screening Checklist

During resume screening, recruiters should verify:

- Relevant education
- Required technical skills
- Years of professional experience
- Employment history
- Certifications
- Career progression
- Resume quality

Candidates missing mandatory qualifications should not proceed to interviews.

---

# RP-102 CV Quality Indicators

Strong resumes usually include:

- Clear career progression
- Measurable achievements
- Relevant technical skills
- Well-organized formatting
- Recent professional experience

Weak resumes often contain:

- Employment gaps without explanation
- Generic job descriptions
- Missing technical details
- Spelling or grammar mistakes
- Inconsistent dates

---

# RP-103 Candidate Communication Standards

Recruiters should:

- Respond within two business days.
- Maintain a professional tone.
- Clearly explain the recruitment process.
- Confirm interview schedules.
- Inform candidates about application outcomes.

Recruiters should never promise employment before HR approval.

---

# RP-104 Technical Skill Evaluation

Recruiters are not expected to perform deep technical assessments.

Instead, recruiters should verify whether candidates claim experience with the required technologies and ensure that technical interviews are scheduled with qualified interviewers.

---

# RP-105 Interview Preparation

Before every interview, recruiters should prepare:

- Candidate resume
- Job description
- Candidate match score
- Previous recruiter notes
- Interview schedule

Preparation improves interview consistency and candidate experience.

---

# RP-106 Candidate Red Flags

Recruiters should document and investigate:

- Frequent job hopping
- Large unexplained employment gaps
- False certifications
- Inconsistent employment dates
- Poor communication
- Refusal to provide references

A red flag does not automatically reject a candidate but requires additional review.

---

# RP-107 Recruiter Best Practices

Recruiters should:

- Evaluate candidates objectively.
- Follow company hiring policies.
- Record interview feedback promptly.
- Escalate uncertain decisions to HR Managers.
- Protect candidate confidentiality.

---

# RP-108 Escalation Guidelines

Recruiters must escalate applications when:

- Match score is borderline.
- Education does not clearly match the role.
- Interview feedback conflicts.
- Background verification raises concerns.
- Hiring managers disagree.

Escalation decisions are reviewed by HR Managers.

---

# RP-109 Recruitment Timeline

Recommended recruitment timeline:

Resume Screening
1–2 business days

Interview Scheduling
Within 3 business days

Interview Process
Within 10 business days

HR Decision
Within 2 business days

Offer Letter
Within 2 business days after approval

---

# RP-110 Common Recruiter Mistakes

Recruiters should avoid:

- Skipping resume verification
- Ignoring interview feedback
- Relying only on match scores
- Sharing confidential candidate information
- Approving candidates without HR authorization
